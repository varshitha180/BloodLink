Blood Donation Eligibility

Before donating blood, make sure you meet the basic eligibility requirements.

🎂

Age

Generally 18 years or older.

⚖️

Health

Donor should be in good general health.

🩸

Blood Group

Know your blood group before donation.

📅

Donation Interval

Follow the recommended interval between donations.

Donation Process

1

Registration

Provide your basic information and complete the registration process.

2

Health Check

A basic health check is performed before donation.

3

Blood Donation

The blood donation process is carried out by trained medical staff.

4

Recovery

Rest and take refreshments after donating blood.

Why Donate Blood?

❤️

Save Lives

Donated blood can help patients during emergencies and medical treatments.

🤝

Help Your Community

Your contribution can support people in your local community.

🏥

Support Hospitals

Blood donations help maintain blood availability for patients.
